## Scene checklist

- One claim, one dominant visual, one intended emotion
- Reading time fits the scene duration
- Headline and evidence are visible before the audience needs them
- Important objects never leave the safe content area
- Exit and next entry vectors agree, or the seam is an intentional hard cut
- Static hero frame remains complete without animation
